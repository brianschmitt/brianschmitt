# Brian Schmitt

**Staff Solutions Architect, Twilio Professional Services**

I help enterprise teams architect and ship production communication systems - voice, messaging, contact centers, identity verification. My clients are household-name brands across fintech, retail, healthcare, and rideshare.

Before Twilio, I spent 20 years as a hands-on architect and dev lead across Fortune 500s (Air Products, PPL Electric), insurance, pharma, and my own consultancy. I've shipped everything from award-winning call center platforms to global B2C billing systems to mobile workforce apps.

I build things that work today and scale tomorrow.
